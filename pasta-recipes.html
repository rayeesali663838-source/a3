Pantry pasta recipes for every night

Five quick, dependable pasta dinners built on cupboard staples. Each serves four and is ready in about half an hour.

Vegan · 25 minutes

Penne all’arrabbiata

Serves 4 · 25 minutes

“Arrabbiata” means angry, a nod to the chilli heat. Adjust the spice to suit your table.

Ingredients

  • 320 g penne
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • ½–1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 400 g canned chopped tomatoes
  • Chopped parsley

Method

  1. Gently cook the garlic and chilli in the oil until fragrant.
  2. Add the tomatoes, season and simmer for 15 minutes until thick.
  3. Toss with al dente penne and a splash of pasta water; finish with parsley.
Vegan · 15 minutes

Aglio, olio e peperoncino

Serves 4 · 15 minutes

Just garlic, oil, chilli and pasta water, emulsified into a silky coating. Simple, but technique matters.

Ingredients

  • 320 g spaghetti
  • 6 tbsp good olive oil
  • 5 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • Pinch of chilli flakes
  • Handful of parsley, chopped

Method

  1. Start the garlic in cold oil and warm slowly until pale gold.
  2. Add the chilli, then a ladle of pasta water to stop the cooking.
  3. Add drained spaghetti and toss vigorously until the sauce turns creamy; finish with parsley.
Contains fish · 20 minutes

Pasta al tonno

Serves 4 · 20 minutes

A store-cupboard favourite across southern Italy. Use tuna packed in olive oil for the best flavour.

Ingredients

  • 320 g pasta
  • 2 cans tuna in olive oil, drained
  • 400 g canned chopped tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp capers and chopped parsley

Method

  1. Cook the garlic in olive oil, add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  2. Stir in the capers and tuna, breaking it into flakes, and warm through.
  3. Toss with the pasta and finish with parsley and a drizzle of oil.
Vegetarian option · 15 minutes

Pasta al limone

Serves 4 · 15 minutes

Bright and silky, this lemon pasta is ready in the time it takes the water to boil and the pasta to cook.

Ingredients

  • 320 g spaghetti or linguine
  • 40 g butter
  • Zest and juice of 1–2 lemons
  • 60 g finely grated hard cheese
  • Black pepper and basil

Method

  1. Melt the butter with the lemon zest and a ladle of pasta water.
  2. Add the drained pasta, then the cheese a little at a time, tossing constantly.
  3. Add lemon juice to taste, plenty of pepper and torn basil.
Vegan · 20 minutes

Spaghetti with garlic pangrattato

Serves 4 · 20 minutes

Known as the “poor man’s parmesan”, toasted breadcrumbs add crunch and richness without cheese.

Ingredients

  • 320 g spaghetti
  • 80 g coarse breadcrumbs
  • 6 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves and a pinch of chilli
  • Lemon zest and parsley

Method

  1. Toast the breadcrumbs in 2 tbsp oil until golden; season and set aside.
  2. Gently cook garlic and chilli in the remaining oil; add pasta and a splash of water.
  3. Toss, then scatter with crumbs, lemon zest and parsley just before serving.

Al dente

Taste the pasta a minute or two before the packet time; it should be tender with a slight bite.

Finish in the pan

Toss pasta and sauce together over heat with a splash of pasta water for a glossy finish.

Allergens

Recipes may contain wheat, fish and dairy. Check labels and use swaps to suit your diet.
