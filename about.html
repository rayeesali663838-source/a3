About Puttanesca Glow

A home-cooking website for anyone who loves big flavours, quick dinners and the warm glow of an evening around the table.

Our story

An almost-empty cupboard

Puttanesca Glow began on a late evening with an almost-empty fridge: a can of tomatoes, a jar of olives, some capers and a packet of spaghetti. Twenty minutes later, dinner was one of the best meals of the week.

That night started a habit of collecting pantry pasta recipes and learning the techniques that make them shine. This website shares them, with clear instructions and honest tips.

Tested at home

Every recipe is cooked in an ordinary home kitchen, often many times, before we publish it.

Pantry-first

We focus on ingredients you can keep in the cupboard, so a good dinner is always within reach.

Clearly labelled

We mark vegan recipes and note fish, dairy and other common allergens.

How we work

Respectful and practical

Our recipes are inspired by southern Italian home cooking. We describe them as inspired rather than authentic, respect regional traditions and note where versions differ. Cooking guidance follows recognised food safety advice.
